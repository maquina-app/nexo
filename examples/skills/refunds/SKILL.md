# Refunds

Follow this policy exactly. Look the order up first; never decide from what the
customer says alone.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Delivered 30 days ago or less, and the customer reports a defect or wrong item | Refund the full amount |
| Delivered 30 days ago or less, and the customer changed their mind | Refund the amount minus a 10% restocking fee |
| Delivered more than 30 days ago | Do not refund. Explain the 30-day window. |
| Not delivered yet | Do not refund. Tell them the expected delivery date. |
| The order cannot be found | Ask for the order number again. Do not guess. |

- Issue at most one refund per order.
- A refund over $200 is unusual: page the on-call lead as well, saying why.
- If a refund is declined by a human, tell the customer it is being reviewed.
  Do not try again.

Reply to the customer in three sentences or fewer, stating the amount and why.
